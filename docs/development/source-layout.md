# JustSessions source layout

[Back to JustSessions](../../README.md) · [Build and release](build-and-release.md)

Application paths below are relative to `Sources/JustSessions/`. Tests live in `Tests/JustSessionsTests/`, with Gherkin features and their steps in `Tests/JustSessionsTests/Gherkin/`; see [Gherkin features](build-and-release.md#gherkin-features).

- `Models/Activity/`: what a running CLI is doing, and a project's running CLIs summed up. `UnseenTurns/` holds the CLIs that finished a turn while their terminal was off screen, until you look, and which running CLIs wait on you: stopped for your answer, or done with a turn you have not seen.
- `Models/Appearance/`: the app's System, Light, or Dark appearance.
- `Models/Appearance/Themes/`: the named themes, with each theme's light and dark colors in `ThemeColors/`, the CLI and status hues every theme shares in `Hues/`, and in `ReadableText/` the text colors each theme prepares so text stays at 4.5 contrast on every surface it sits on. `Customization/` holds your changes to a theme's colors, which `ResolvedAppTheme` applies to its light and dark colors, and the check that refuses a surface color text can't stay readable on.
- `Models/Conversations/`: the session model, its CLI, and the New, Resume, and Branch actions.
- `Models/Transcript/`: the reading width, a readable column or the full window, and the reading text size, both shared by the preview and every reading window.
- `Models/Transcript/Markdown/`: parsed prose, fenced code, and table blocks used by conversation reading.
- `Models/Customizations/`: session and project names you set, in `Pins/` the pinned projects and sessions, each in the order you put them, in `Archive/` the sessions archived out of the Projects list, and in `Bookmarks/` each session's bookmarked messages with their letters, labels, and tags, what a bookmark search finds (`MessageBookmarkQuery`), and what All Bookmarks lists (`AllBookmarksList`).
- `Models/Customizations/DeletedWhenTabCloses/`, `Services/Deletion/WhenTabCloses/`, `Views/Browser/NewSession/DeleteWhenClosed/`: **Delete the session when its tab closes**, a box in the New session sheet. The sessions started so, kept in settings; the tab passing its choice to the session it links to; and closing its tab, which deletes the session as Close and delete does, without asking.
- `Models/StartCommands/`: the command each tool's CLI starts with on each host, when it is not the one the app uses, set in the New session sheet; and what that command stands in for, `kiro-cli chat` for Kiro CLI and the executable for the other tools.
- `Models/Onboarding/`: the onboarding tour's stops, the tips a fresh install shows once each, and when each tip is due from what the window shows.
- `Models/RandomTips/`: the tips the bottom of the sidebar picks from, and which comes next: a random one not shown yet, so every tip shows once before any repeats.
- `Models/Notifications/`: which CLIs just finished a turn or stopped to wait on you, what their notification says, and which moments notify. `Bell/` holds the notification bell's rows and when each CLI started waiting on you, for their order and ages.
- `Models/Permissions/`: the macOS permissions the app depends on, and what macOS says about each.
- `Models/Hosts/`: this Mac and saved SSH hosts, project locations and keys, each host's refresh status and the tool an SSH host's copy is on, and the SSH hosts whose tmux sessions use the host's own prefix keys.
- `Models/Sidebar/`: the sidebar's filters, projects with their sessions, multi-selection, which Open tabs groups are collapsed and which of its rows are selected to close together, in `Projects/ShowMoreSessions/`, how many of a project's sessions are listed before **Show N more**, in `Subagents/`, which sessions list their subagents' sessions under them, in `Reordering/`, where a project or session dragged among the pinned ones lands, and in `DraggingIntoSplit/`, the session or open tab a row dragged onto the terminals brings.
- `Services/Sidebar/`: the sidebar's projects as the filters and search leave them. `Sessions/` picks what a session row shows as its CLI's status: its running tab's, the selected one first, then its CLI's in tmux with no tab open.
- `Models/Terminal/Appearance/`: terminal colors, font, and size, and the terminal part of a theme's colors.
- `Models/Terminal/Appearance/ColorSchemes/`: which colors terminals use, the app theme's, a preset, or imported ones, with the colors of presets that are not app themes, Dracula and Nord, in `Presets/`.
- `Models/Terminal/Appearance/ColorIntensity/`: the **Color intensity** setting, which makes the 16 ANSI colors more or less vivid by scaling their OKLab chroma at the same lightness and hue.
- `Models/Terminal/Engine/`: the engines that can draw a tab's terminal: SwiftTerm by default, or Ghostty when chosen in Appearance settings.
- `Models/Terminal/Tabs/`: where tabs open and which shows after one closes, so each project's tabs stay together; tab groups and their colors.
- `Models/Terminal/Tabs/HoverCard/`, `Views/Browser/Tabs/HoverCard/`: the card under a tab or group label the pointer rests on, as in Chrome. A tab's shows its whole title, project, CLI, and status; a group's lists its tabs. The models decide when it shows and hides; the views draw it over the terminal.
- `Models/Terminal/Tabs/Moving/`: dragging in the tab bar: a tab moves within its group, a split's two tabs together, and a group whole by its label; and where a dragged tab or group lands as the pointer moves.
- `Models/Terminal/Tabs/Moving/BetweenWindows/`: taking a tab, a split's two tabs, or a whole group's tabs out of a window's tabs whole, and where they go among another window's tabs or groups.
- `Models/Terminal/Closing/`: what closing a tab does when its CLI can keep running in tmux: ask each time, keep it running, or end it; and whether closing a plain terminal asks first.
- `Models/Terminal/Closing/SeveralTabs/`: the one question Close all tabs in group…, or closing the tabs selected in Open tabs, asks for all the tabs: each tab's need, checked in the order a single tab's close checks it, and what they need together, with the saved choices answering only when no tab would stop without asking. `Services/Terminal/Closing/SeveralTabs/` gives each tab its need and closes the tabs, the selected one last, so a tab left open shows.
- `Models/Terminal/Split/`: the pairs of tabs linked in split views, how splitting, reversing, separating, swapping, and closing reorder the tabs as Chrome does, with one difference: a split of two projects' tabs is a group of its own between the project groups, under a key of the split's own, so neither tab leaves its project for the other's; where a shown split's panes and the resize area between them sit, side by side or stacked, which side a tab dropped on the terminals takes, or the whole area with no tab shown to split with (`Dropping/`), and the split entries a tab's menus offer. `Services/Terminal/Split/` keeps which splits are stacked, picks a tab's entries and does what each one asks, including a new session opened straight into a split, for the tab bar's menu and the terminal's, and shows a tab, or a session or tab dragged from the sidebar, dropped on the terminals in a split (`Dropping/`), resuming a session with no tab open first. `Choosing/` in both is **Add Tab to New Split View** (⌥⌘N), as Chrome's split view page: a chooser beside the selected tab, which tabs it lists and in what order, and choosing, reversing, or closing it; a tab put on its side takes its place.
- `Models/Wording/`: counts and relative times in labels.
- `Models/TextZoom/`, `Services/TextZoom/`, `Views/TextZoom/`: View → Actual Size, Zoom In, and Zoom Out (⌘0, ⌘+ or ⌘=, ⌘−), as in Chrome. They size the terminals' font while a tab shows, otherwise the conversation's text in the preview or a reading window.
- `Services/Store/`: `ConversationStore`, the state the views observe. Each feature extends it from its own folder.
- `Services/Windows/`: the open workspace windows, each with its store, so a session runs in one tab across them. Resuming a session another window's tab runs brings that window forward to the tab, and each window's session rows, project summaries, Running and Waiting for you filters, and activity sync count other windows' tabs as the session's own.
- `Services/Windows/MovingTabs/`: moves a session's tab here from another window when tmux keeps its CLI running: that tab closes and a tab here reattaches. Over SSH, the host first confirms its tmux runs the CLI.
- `Services/Windows/DraggingTabs/`: moves dragged tabs from one window's store to another's as they are, their terminals and CLIs still running, and keeps each window's selection and saved tabs right.
- `Services/Activity/`: reads what each CLI on this Mac is doing, from Claude Code's live registry, Codex session files, Antigravity's conversation databases, and the reports of the app's Pi and OpenCode extensions. `Codex/AppServer/` asks Codex's shared background server, over its Unix socket, which threads wait for your approval. `UnseenTurns/` marks a session after each sync and clears the mark once its tab is on screen.
- `Services/Notifications/`: posts notifications through macOS, opens the session a clicked one or a bell row is about, and saves which moments notify. `Bell/` lists the sessions waiting for you across the windows for the sidebar's bell, and times each wait after the activity sync.
- `Services/Onboarding/`: saves which onboarding tips have shown, and whether the install shows them at all, decided once from whether it was fresh; Help's Take the tour shows them all again.
- `Services/RandomTips/`: whether the sidebar shows a random tip, set in Settings → Help, the tip this launch shows, and the tips shown so far. A launch that shows the onboarding tour shows no random tip.
- `Services/Permissions/`: reads each permission's status without asking for it, and the System Settings page that changes it.
- `Services/Startup/`: registers the app as a macOS login item and reads its current system approval status.
- `Services/CrashReports/`: at launch, finds the crash macOS recorded for the app since the last launch, in `~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`, and offers once to report it.
- `Services/Appearance/`: saves the app's appearance, theme, and your changes to each theme's colors, sets the appearance on every window, and gives terminals a newly chosen theme's colors.
- `Services/Adapters/`: provider discovery and native arguments, one folder per CLI. `Kiro/` reads V2's `sessions/cli` and V3's session folders (`KiroV3Session`), and the lock Kiro keeps on a session while a CLI runs it (`KiroSessionLock`). Separate adapters make adding another CLI straightforward.
- `Services/Hosts/`: refreshing every host and starting new sessions on any of them.
- `Services/Hosts/ThisMacFolder/`: checks a folder typed for a new session or project on this Mac, and creates a missing one once the sheet's offer is taken; `Services/Remote/NewSession/RemoteFolderResolver` does the same on an SSH host.
- `Services/Hosts/InstalledCLIs/`: which CLIs each host has, so new sessions offer only those.
- `Services/Hosts/AutoRefresh/`: refreshes the hosts on their own while a workspace window shows on screen: this Mac every minute, each SSH host every 5 minutes, less often after failures, and when the app comes forward. The oldest window copies an SSH host.
- `Services/Launch/`: CLI executable resolution and process environment, and resuming a session, which takes an archived one out of the archive.
- `Services/Launch/PlainTerminal/`: opens a plain terminal, a login shell in a project folder that is no session.
- `Services/Launch/MoveToFolder/`, `Models/Conversations/MoveToFolder/`, `Views/SessionActions/MoveToFolder/`: a Claude Code session's **Move to**, which resumes it with Claude Code's `/cd <folder>` as its first message, so the CLI moves the session; and the projects the menu offers.
- `Services/Launch/StartCommand/`: runs a start command of your own through a shell, on this Mac and on SSH hosts, with the app's arguments after it; `env` then runs the CLI in the shell's place, so the tab's process is still the CLI's. `Check/` reads a command before it is kept, without running it, and looks up its program where the CLI runs. The New session sheet's field, locked until Edit, is in `Views/Browser/NewSession/StartCommand/`.
- `Services/Remote/`: SSH mirroring, commands on the host, new sessions and folder lookup, deletion, and tmux there, including its prefix keys and, for tmux before 3.6, attaching a Claude Code tab's client again after a light/dark change so the CLI redraws in the new colors.
- `Services/Remote/Mirror/Folders/`: asks the host's login shell for the variables and Pi setting that move each CLI's folder, works the folders out with this Mac's adapter rules, and keeps them beside the host's copy so deletion uses the same folders.
- `Services/Remote/HostStatus/`: one SSH call per refresh that lists the host's tmux sessions and installed CLIs.
- `Services/Remote/CLIActivity/`: how the app reads what a CLI on an SSH host does from the title the tab's tmux session shows. `ClaudeCode/` holds the relay a Claude Code tab's tmux session starts on the host, and the decoder of its base64 registry and custom-title records, which carry activity, session ids, and names in that title; `LiveSessionReporting/` writes Pi's and OpenCode's extension to the host and starts the CLI with it, and the extension's `LiveSessionTmuxActivity` part sets the title.
- `Services/Remote/CLILocation/`: where each CLI on an SSH host starts from, as the last refresh found it: by name in the login shell, which runs an alias or function there and `exec`s a program, or by its path in an install folder that shell's PATH leaves out.
- `Services/Remote/SharedCopy/`: once one window has copied an SSH host, the other windows list that copy instead of copying the host again.
- `Services/Remote/ConnectionSharing/`: lets one remote-session refresh's commands and copies share one app-owned SSH login, then closes it, unless the host's SSH config supplies its own control path or turns sharing off, or the host is logged in to with Connect….
- `Services/Remote/Login/`: Connect…, which logs in to a host that asks for a password, a passphrase, or a code, and keeps that login open, with no idle limit, so every command and tab on the host goes through it; a login an app that crashed left open is closed at the next launch. `Askpass/` is the helper `ssh` runs to ask each question: the app's own executable, started with the login's socket, hands the question to the app and prints the answer. The question sheet and the heading's Connect… button are in `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Hosts/Login/`.
- `Services/Remote/Disconnect/`: Disconnect, in a host's ⋯ and right-click menus, after which the app runs nothing on the host on its own until Connect or using the host connects it again; the host's tabs close in every window first. The kept choice is `Models/Hosts/Disconnect/`; the question about the tabs and the heading's **Disconnected** label are in `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Hosts/Disconnect/`.
- `Services/Remote/PortForwarding/`: Forward a port…: the ports an SSH host listens on that `localhost` reaches, and the forwards from this Mac, which go through the host's Connect… login or one `ssh -N` connection the app opens for them, are added to it one by one without `~/.ssh/config`'s, and are added again when it reconnects; a connection an app that crashed left is closed at the next launch.
- `Services/Remote/Reconnect/`: reconnects SSH tabs whose connection dropped, as when the Mac slept, once their host lists their tmux session again. The host is asked after the drop, when the Mac wakes, the app comes forward, or Connect… logs in, then less often for 15 minutes; a tab whose tmux session is gone stays ended. The ended bar says **Connection lost. Reconnecting…** meanwhile.
- `Services/Remote/KeystrokeTiming/`: turns off OpenSSH's 20 ms keystroke batches for tabs and the Connect… login they go through, when this Mac's `ssh` takes the option.
- `Services/Remote/SSHConfiguration/`: reads the settings `ssh` would use for a host with `ssh -G`, without connecting, and finds a listed host that connects to the same account and address. The Add SSH host sheet reads what is typed with `Models/Hosts/HostInput/` and shows why it can't be added in `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Hosts/AddHost/`.
- `Services/Remote/Reachability/`: remembers for a minute a host the background commands couldn't reach, so the next ones fail at once instead of waiting for `ConnectTimeout`; a refresh you ask for, Connect…, a network change, or the Mac waking tries it again.
- `Services/Remote/ConnectionCheck/`: logs in to a host before the Add SSH host sheet adds it, so the sheet can say why the host can't be reached, and tells a Windows host apart (`WindowsHostProbe`), which a refresh also asks when the host's shell ran none of its folder lookup.
- `Services/Remote/ShellStartup/`: how every command starts the host's login shell, with `JUSTSESSIONS=1`, and the check that finds startup files that start another program, such as tmux or zsh, before the app's command. Such a host falls back to a login shell without its interactive startup; the heading's warning and ⋯ items are in `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Hosts/`.
- `Services/Remote/HostCheck/`: Check this host…, from a host's ⋯ menu, its failed refresh's warning, or an ended tab's Check host. It logs in, checks the shell startup, then runs one read-only script on the host that looks up `rsync`, tmux, each CLI, `python3`, and the tab's folder; `Findings/` reads the answers into findings, each with what the host printed and a command to run in Terminal. `EndedTab/` reads the ended tab's last lines into the report. `Background/` checks hosts on their own, on the auto refresher's beat, and keeps each host's last check for its heading's warning. `FixAgent/` starts Fix with agent…'s CLI with a prompt made of the report, the host's facts, and sections of `docs/guides`, which the app ships through the `Resources/Guides` link. The report, the check's steps, and the agent's form state are `Models/Hosts/HostCheck/`, the sheet and form `Views/Browser/HostCheck/`.
- `Services/Remote/NewSession/ClaudeSessionID/`: asks an SSH host whether its `claude` takes `--session-id`, so a new tab there starts with its session's id and is linked by it.
- `Services/Tmux/ThisMac/`: bundled runtime discovery, terminal database environment, compatible server selection, and local persistence.
- `Cloudflare/Feedback/`: the Worker that stores feedback sent from the Guide's form, which **Settings → Send feedback** opens in the browser, after checking its Cloudflare Turnstile token. See [Feedback](website.md#feedback).
- `Cloudflare/UpdateFeed/`: the Worker at the app's `SUFeedURL`, which counts update checks per day and app version and redirects to the appcast on GitHub. See [Counting update checks](update-checks.md).
- `Scripts/Release/`: release checks, such as opening the packaged app without the build machine's resource bundles.
- `Scripts/Version/`: the version a local build shows, read from `git describe`.
- `Scripts/Tmux/`: pinned source builds, license collection, and relocated runtime verification for app packaging and CI.
- `Scripts/DemoVideo/`: the homepage demo video: the isolated demo app and its sample sessions, the scripted takes and their recorder, and the edit. See [Demo video](demo-video.md).
- `Services/Tmux/`: tmux session names, and keeping a CLI running after its tab closes, on any host.
- `Services/Tmux/ThisMac/`: this Mac's own tmux server, its version check, finding each tab's CLI process, and keeping a failed CLI's output and exit status in its tab. `ConversationStore+LostKiroCLIs` gives a Kiro CLI a tab left under its temporary name its session's name, using `Models/Terminal/LostKiroCLIs`.
- `Services/Terminal/`: active pseudo-terminal sessions and process lifecycle. `TabTerminalView` is what a tab asks of its terminal, whichever engine draws it.
- `Services/Terminal/Engine/`: saves the engine new tabs open with, chosen in Appearance settings. A tab keeps the engine it opened with, also when an SSH tab reconnects, a session's tab moves to another window, or a waiting tab is remade for a host's new tmux prefix setting. A tab reopened at launch opens with the engine chosen then.
- `Services/Terminal/Ghostty/`: a tab's terminal drawn by Ghostty, which runs the tab's process on a pseudo-terminal of the app's own and hands Ghostty its output, pausing the reading while Ghostty catches up, and the Ghostty controllers whose configuration gives those terminals the appearance settings. Where Ghostty's own behavior differs from a SwiftTerm tab's, the tab keeps SwiftTerm's: its own light and dark theme reports, sent after Ghostty's answer when one follows a background query, the app's right-click menu in place of Ghostty's, Shift-Return and Control keys as CSI u in tmux, the empty paste ⌘V sends with only an image on the clipboard, and Page Up and Page Down, alone or with Shift, which scroll on the main screen and go to a program on the alternate screen. While a program has turned on the kitty keyboard protocol, Ghostty sends the page keys on either screen, as SwiftTerm does.
- `Services/Terminal/Ghostty/Appearance/`: the terminal appearance settings as Ghostty configuration and a light and dark theme, with the keys that make a Ghostty terminal behave as a SwiftTerm one.
- `Services/Terminal/Ghostty/Appearance/Font/`: the font keys that make Ghostty draw text as SwiftTerm does: the faces `NSFontManager` gives SwiftTerm for bold and italic, the fallback families macOS draws missing characters with, unscaled, the bundled symbols font for Nerd Font icons the terminal font lacks, placed ahead of the fallback families that draw other glyphs at icons' codepoints, cells adjusted to SwiftTerm's pixel size, and strokes thickened as much as SwiftTerm's font smoothing thickens them for the theme's text color. Wide characters still sit a point or two left of where SwiftTerm centers them.
- `Models/Terminal/Reopening/ClosedTabs/`, `Services/Terminal/Reopening/ClosedTabs/`: File → Reopen Closed Tab (⇧⌘T). `closeTerminal(_:)` keeps each closed tab in its window's `RecentlyClosedTabs`, the latest 25, with its place in the tab bar, except tabs closed to delete their session, moved to another window, or closed by disconnecting their host (`closingTabsWithoutRemembering`).
- `Services/Terminal/Closing/`: waits for a closed tab's process to exit, so it leaves no zombie behind, and saves what closing a tab whose CLI can keep running does on each host, and whether closing a plain terminal asks first, chosen with Don't ask again in the close dialog or in General settings.
- `Models/Terminal/Find/`, `Services/Terminal/Find/`, `Views/Terminal/Find/`: ⌘F in a tab. `TerminalFindTarget` picks what it searches: a linked session's conversation, in a reading window, or the terminal. `TerminalTextFinding` is the search each engine gives: SwiftTerm's in `SelectableTerminalView+Find`, with a match count, and Ghostty's in `Ghostty/GhosttyTabTerminalView+Find`, through its search binding actions and without a count. The view folder has the find bar that floats over the terminal, each tab's find state, and the find keys, which take Return and Escape only while the find field has the keyboard.
- `Models/Terminal/ConversationMap/`, `Services/Terminal/ConversationMap/`, `Views/Terminal/ConversationMap/`: View → Show Conversation Map. `ConversationMapChipTitles` cuts bookmarks' labels to fit their chips, and `ConversationMapScrollReader` scrolls a long conversation's map. Each CLI tab's outline of its session (`ConversationOutlineModel`, held by the tab), read off the main thread by `ConversationOutlineReader` with the reader's decoder and extended from its last record as the file grows; the pure layout that fits it to the column's height and its contrast-checked colors; the column, its one Canvas, hover card, clicks into the reading window, the window-visibility reader that stops it out of sight, and the menu item.
- `Views/Terminal/Bookmarks/`: ⌘D, ⌘', and ⇧⌘' in a tab linked to a session (`TerminalTabBookmarks`, a second target for `TranscriptBookmarkTargetRegistry`), and the notice over the terminal's corner.
- `Services/Terminal/FileDrop/`: files dropped on a tab type their paths, each as its own paste, so Claude Code and Codex attach a dropped image. `SSHHost/` copies files dropped on a tab on an SSH host there first, one at a time, and types the host's paths once all are there; `Services/Remote/DroppedFiles/` copies one file over `ssh` into a private folder in `~/.cache/justsessions/dropped-files` on the host, and `Views/Terminal/FileDrop/` shows the copy's progress, or why it failed, over the tab's corner.
- `Services/Terminal/ImagePaste/`: ⌘V or the right-click menu's Paste with only an image on the clipboard. A tab on this Mac sends an empty bracketed paste, which Claude Code answers by reading the image off the clipboard itself: SwiftTerm's own paste sends one, and a Ghostty tab, whose engine drops an empty paste, sends it while the program has bracketed paste on (`Ghostty/GhosttyTabTerminalView+ImagePaste`, which overrides `AppTerminalView`'s Paste; see [libghostty](build-and-release.md#libghostty)). A tab on an SSH host saves the image in a private temporary folder and hands it to `FileDrop/SSHHost/`, which copies it there as a dropped file, types its host path, and removes the saved image once the copy ends.
- `Services/Terminal/Links/`: what ⌘-clicking a link in a tab opens, whichever engine found it: a web page or email address as given, or a file or folder on this Mac, a relative path in the tab's project folder and without a trailing line number, in the app macOS opens it with. A file goes instead where **Settings → General → Tabs** says (`Models/Terminal/Links/FileLinkClickChoice`, saved by `FileLinkClickSettingsStore`): the link's menu at the pointer, or an editor for the text files `FileLinkEditorScope` takes. `Views/Terminal/Links/TerminalLinkActions` holds the link items a right-click shows and what a ⌘-click does with the window's store, which shows an editor's error; the tab's view in its window gives its terminal that ⌘-click. Nothing opens that would run, such as an app or a `.command` script, and a tab on an SSH host opens no paths. SwiftTerm hands links over through `requestOpenLink`, and Ghostty through its open-URL delegate; a Ghostty tab adds Shift to a ⌘-click while a program such as tmux takes the mouse, so Ghostty finds the link there as SwiftTerm does. This Mac's tmux passes OSC 8 hyperlinks on to the tab (`-T hyperlinks`). A right-click finds the link under the pointer the same way (`link(under:)`) for the menu's link items; Ghostty reports a link only while ⌘ is held, so its tab moves the pointer there once more with ⌘ to ask. A CLI breaks a long URL or path over rows itself, which Ghostty finds a row at a time, so while a program draws its own screen a Ghostty tab joins a word that reaches the edge with the next row's first word (`Links/HardWrapped/`, `GhosttyTabTerminalView+HardWrappedLinks`); SwiftTerm joins such rows itself. `Services/ExternalEditors/LocalFiles/` opens a linked file in an installed editor, at its line in a VS Code-family editor through `code --goto`.
- `Services/Terminal/ControlKeys/`: keys with Control whose legacy byte also stands for another key, such as Ctrl-/ and Ctrl-_, or that SwiftTerm sends nothing for, such as Ctrl-Return. A tab whose CLI runs in this Mac's tmux sends them as CSI u, which tmux passes to a CLI that asks for modifyOtherKeys 2 and turns into the legacy byte for others; `Ghostty/GhosttyTabTerminalView+ControlKeys` does the same for a Ghostty tab. Without tmux a SwiftTerm tab sends the legacy bytes kitty and Ghostty send. With an input method's full-width punctuation, the key's US character counts.
- `Services/Terminal/Screen/`: a terminal's screen as rows of text with the cell each character starts at, wide characters taking two, which links a CLI broke over rows and the prompt selection read.
- `Services/Terminal/PromptSelection/`: ⌘A in a CLI's tab selects only the text in its prompt. `Recognizers/` finds each CLI's prompt on the screen with the caret in it (`CLIPromptLayout`), `Prompt/` joins its rows into the text ⌘C copies, and `Keys/` turns the key you press into the keys the CLI reads to delete the prompt or go to either end. `TerminalPromptSelection` draws the selection over the terminal and ends it on any other key, a click, or the prompt changing; each engine reads its screen for it, in `SelectableTerminalView+PromptSelection` and `Ghostty/GhosttyTabTerminalView+PromptSelection`.
- `Services/Terminal/Appearance/`: saves terminal colors, font, and size, and applies them with the theme's colors to every terminal, with a minimum contrast that keeps text readable when a CLI colored it for the other background, and ANSI colors moved off the background so a fill in any of them shows.
- `Services/Terminal/Appearance/Fonts/`: the installed monospaced families the font picker offers, those whose letters fill a terminal's cells, which SwiftTerm makes as wide as the font's W, and the bundled Nerd Font symbols every font but System Monospaced falls back to for icons.
- `Services/Terminal/Appearance/ThemeReports/`: answers a program that asks whether the terminal is light or dark (`CSI ? 996 n`), and tells one that subscribes (`CSI ? 2031 h`), such as tmux, each time the terminal's colors change. SwiftTerm handles neither; on each report tmux reads the new background, so a CLI in tmux sees it. A report can also follow the terminal's answer to the next background query (`OSC 11 ; ?`), for tmux before 3.6, which does not subscribe.
- `Services/Terminal/Appearance/Import/`: reads colors from iTerm2's default profile or an `.itermcolors` file, only when asked.
- `Services/Terminal/NewSessionDiscovery/`: finds the session a new tab's CLI is writing and links the tab to it.
- `Services/Terminal/ClaudeLiveSession/`: reads Claude Code's live registry on this Mac, or its relayed record in an SSH tab's title, for names chosen with `/rename`, and makes a tab follow its CLI to the session `/clear` starts on its own host.
- `Services/Terminal/AppendedSessionNames/`: shows a name an open tab's CLI on this Mac appends to a file as soon as it does: Claude Code's `/rename` and Pi's `/name` in the session's own file, and a Codex thread's name in `session_index.jsonl`. For Claude Code it backs up the live registry, which some setups leave without the name.
- `Services/Terminal/CodexLiveThread/`: starts Codex on this Mac with its thread's id in the terminal title, and reads that title so a new tab finds its thread and a tab follows its CLI to the thread `/new`, `/clear`, `/resume`, or `/fork` moves it to.
- `Services/Terminal/LiveSessionFollowing/`: makes an Antigravity, Pi, or OpenCode tab on this Mac follow the session its CLI is in: Antigravity's from the log it holds open, in `Antigravity/`; Pi's and OpenCode's from the extension the app starts them with, which reports it to a file, in `Reporting/`, along with what the CLI is doing.
- `Services/Terminal/Tabs/`: moving between tabs, and linking a tab to the session its CLI is in.
- `Services/Terminal/AppearingSessions/`: links a new tab to the first session that appears in its project, for SSH hosts and for CLIs that don't reveal the session they write.
- `Services/Processes/`: process tree, open files, and short helper processes with a timeout.
- `Services/Processes/LoginShell/`: reads the environment your login shell sets up, once per app run. CLI lookup takes its `PATH`, and every `ssh` and `rsync` runs with all of it (`Services/Remote/Environment/`).
- `Services/Transcript/`: read-only conversation readers for the preview, and `TranscriptImageDecoder`, which decodes each image once for every reader that shows it.
- `Services/Transcript/Markdown/`: splits Markdown into prose, code blocks, and tables for the native views.
- `Views/Browser/`: window layout, with folders for the sidebar, the terminal tab bar, and the New session sheet.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Groups/`: a project's tab group in the tab bar: its colored label and its tabs; and a split across two projects, a container with no label holding its two tabs, each in its project's color.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Menus/`: the right-click menus of the tab bar and of Open tabs, which share them: a tab's, which starts with Rename and what concerns its session; a group label's or heading's, which starts with New session in its project and collapses groups or closes the group's tabs; and the empty space's, with New session… and collapsing or expanding every group. `Views/Terminal/ContextMenu/` gives a terminal the tab's items in AppKit.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Closing/`: the dialogs closing a tab, or all of a group's tabs, asks before it stops a CLI or shell.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Dragging/`: the drag gesture tabs and group labels share, which takes over from their buttons once the pointer moves.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Dragging/Display/`: how a drag along the tab bar is drawn. A move of the pointer re-runs only each tab's or group's shift; the tabs, their groups, and the bar re-run only as the dragged one passes another. Each window has its own displays for tabs dragged in from another window, so no other window's bar redraws.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Dragging/BetweenWindows/`: dragging tabs, or a whole group by its label, out of the tab bar, as in Chrome: past the bar they leave in a window of their own that follows the pointer, and over another window's bar, or back over theirs, they join it. Each bar reports where its groups and tabs are, so the tabs arrive where its drag then keeps them. Over a window's terminals, a tab that can show in a split there hides the window carrying it and lights up the pane it would take.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Dragging/IntoSplit/`: dropping a dragged tab into a split view, as Claude Code drops a session into its window: where each window's terminals are, and the highlight of the pane the tab, or a row dragged from the sidebar, would take, with its title.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/SplitChooser/`: a split chooser's half of the split tab it makes with its tab, titled **Choose a tab**.
- `Views/Browser/Tabs/Appearance/`: the Chrome-style tab shape and sizes, joined for a split's two tabs, how tabs narrow to share the bar as more open, and the terminal colors the selected tab takes on.
- `Views/Browser/WindowDragging/`: the tab bar's window move zone. AppKit would move the window from a press on a tab, since the bar is in the title bar, so while the pointer is over the bar the window can't be moved, and the app moves it itself from the bar's empty space.
- `Views/Browser/Split/`: what a shown split view draws around its panes, as Chrome does: the area's terminal-colored background, each pane's rounded outline with its mini toolbar in the corner, and the resize area between the panes, which you drag to resize them. `Rearranging/` is a pane dragged by its mini toolbar to another edge of the terminals, which moves its tab there, side by side or stacked. `Choosing/` is a split chooser's pane: the list of tabs to show beside the selected tab, picked with a click or ↑, ↓, and Return, or New Session… when there is none.
- `Views/Browser/DockMenu/`: the Dock icon's right-click menu, whose New Window opens a workspace window.
- `Views/Browser/WorkspaceWindows/`: tells the window registry in `Services/Windows/` which window shows each store, so another window can bring it forward, and hands over SwiftUI's `openWindow` as each workspace window appears, for the Dock menu's New Window and for tabs dragged out of their window.
- `Views/Browser/AppWideSheets/`: opens Settings on a workspace window from the sidebar or the app menu, which picks the frontmost workspace window. The Help menu selects Help in the same sheet.
- `Views/Browser/SidebarToggle/`: the title bar button and View menu command that hide or show the sidebar.
- `Views/Browser/TitleBar/`: puts the sidebar toggle, then Back and Forward, beside the window buttons, and gives the title bar's height and where those controls end, which the tab bar lines up with.
- `Models/Navigation/`, `Services/Navigation/`, `Views/Browser/Navigation/`: Back and Forward, as in Chrome, through the tabs and sessions a window showed. The history records every tab or session the window shows, by any means, except that moving between a shown split's two panes changes the current entry; Back and Forward pass over tabs that closed and sessions the sidebar's filters hide. The title bar's arrows list the places they go through on a right-click, and **Go → Back** and **Forward** take ⌘[ and ⌘].
- `Views/Browser/Sidebar/`: the sidebar and its one-line footer, with a folder each for filters, hosts, projects (with `Projects/ShowMoreSessions/`, the **Show N more** row and what keeps a shown session's row listed), session rows, subagents' session rows with the chevron that shows them, and multi-selection. `SidebarRowMoreActionsMenu` is the ⋯ that project and session rows show under the pointer, which opens the row's right-click menu. `Reordering/` holds the drag that moves a project among its host's pinned projects, or a session among its project's pinned sessions, the line that shows where it lands, and the row frames that is worked out from. `DraggingIntoSplit/` holds the drag of a session row, a new session's row, or an Open tabs row onto the window's terminals, which follows the pointer alongside the pin drag and lights up where the row would show.
- `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Header/`: the app mark with the search, notification bell, and new session icons; search opens into a field across that line. `NotificationBell/` draws the bell and the list it opens.
- `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Navigation/`: the Projects / Open tabs switch and persistent view containers that retain both lists' scroll positions.
- `Views/Browser/Sidebar/OpenTabs/`: the full-height open-tab list, grouped by each tab's own project under headings in the tab bar's group colors that collapse their groups, without indenting the rows, each split tab's row marked with its side, ⌘- and Shift-click selection of rows to close together, independent search, and empty states.
- `Views/Browser/Sidebar/Hosts/`: host headings with their own refresh buttons and progress, a warning after the name whose tooltip says why the last refresh failed, and the Add SSH host button. `Login/` holds the Connect… button a host that asks for a password or code shows after its name, and the sheet that asks `ssh`'s questions. `AddHost/` holds its sheet, which logs in to the host before adding it, offering Connect… when the host asks for a password or code, and the note under its field that says why what was typed can't be added. `PortForwarding/` holds the Forward a port sheet and each forward's item in the host's menu. `Menu/` holds a heading's right-click menu, and an SSH host's own settings, which the heading's ⋯ shows alone in place of the refresh status under the pointer, because its + and refresh buttons offer the rest. `AddProject/` holds the heading's +, a menu that also restores archived projects while the host has any, and the sheet that types the path of a project to add on an SSH host and offers to create the folder there when it is missing; this Mac's is picked with the folder panel in `Views/Browser/ProjectFolders/`, which the New session sheet uses too.
- `Views/Terminal/`: a tab's embedded terminal, inset from the window's edges, and the bar above it once its CLI ends, which leads to Check host when the CLI failed right after starting or its host has a problem.
- `Views/Terminal/ContextMenu/`: the terminal's right-click menu, built in AppKit each time it shows: on a link, what to do with it (`TerminalLinkActions`); then Copy, Paste, and Select All; then the tab's own items from the tab bar's menu.
- `Views/Preview/`: conversation preview for the selected session.
- `Views/Preview/MissingFolder/`: the note under a session's header when its project folder is gone from this Mac.
- `Views/Preview/OtherWindow/`: the note under a session's header while a tab in another window runs it, whose **Show** brings that window forward and **Move Here** moves the tab to this window.
- `Views/Preview/Markdown/`: formatted prose, horizontally scrolling code and tables, and code copying. `TranscriptMarkdownTableLayout` fits a table's columns to the reader's column, as a browser does.
- `Views/Preview/Text/`: the AppKit text view every block of transcript text is drawn in, with its style, colors, and Find's highlights, and the bookmark item of a message's right-click menu.
- `Views/Preview/Selection/`: one selection per reader across its text blocks and messages: dragging, Shift-click, Select All, and copying in reading order.
- `Views/Preview/Reading/` and `ReadingPosition/`: text size, reading width, first/latest-message controls, and reading-position restoration.
- `Views/Preview/ReadingWindow/`: one independent, read-only window per host-qualified session; `SessionReadingRequests` asks a new or open window, once, to open Find or go to a bookmark or message.
- `Models/Transcript/Search/`, `Services/Transcript/Search/`, and `Views/Preview/Search/`: temporary per-reader text matching, keyboard shortcuts, highlights, and navigation to occurrences in the displayed transcript.
- `Models/Customizations/Bookmarks/`, `Services/Transcript/Bookmarks/`, and `Views/Preview/Bookmarks/`: message bookmarks: letters a–z per session, saved with the other customizations; finding a bookmarked message across pages; the Bookmarks menu (⌘D, ⌘', ⇧⌘') routed to the key window's reader, or the selected linked tab (`TranscriptBookmarkTargetRegistry`); the reading toolbar's bookmark menu, the letters beside messages with the hover card of a labelled or tagged one, a labelled one's label above its message (`TranscriptBookmarkLabel`), the mark beside the message ⌘D would bookmark (`TranscriptBookmarkTarget`), the notice when a message is gone, Edit Bookmark… (⇧⌘D, `MessageBookmarkEditor`), whose sheet a reader, a tab, or All Bookmarks shows on its own window, and All Bookmarks (⌥⌘B, `AllBookmarksSheet`). The sidebar's search shows bookmarks found by label or tag under their sessions (`Views/Browser/Sidebar/MessageSearch/SidebarSessionMatchDetail`).
- `Models/MessageSearch/`, `Services/MessageSearch/`, and `Views/Browser/Sidebar/MessageSearch/`: searching every listed session's messages from the sidebar. `Text/` reads a whole session the way the reader pages through it, and resumes a grown Codex session from its saved final page (`CodexSessionTextResumer`), `Cache/` keeps that text on disk per session until its file changes, with where a Codex read can resume, and `Matching/` finds a query in it. `SessionMessageIndexer` reads the sessions while a window searches, and the sidebar shows a matching line under each session; clicking one opens the reader at that message with Find showing the search.
- `Views/Dialogs/`: the store's alert, the deletion confirmation, and the Rename dialogs of sessions and projects (`RenameDialogs`), and in `CrashReports/` the offer to report the last crash. `ClickOutside/` closes a sheet, alert, or dialog, as Cancel would, when you click the window around it.
- `Views/Onboarding/`: starts the tour on a fresh install's first window, or from Help, then each tip the first time its part of the window comes into use, such as reading a session, opening a tab, or opening a second one for split view; and the tip card with its copy. `Popover/` shows the card beside the control it is about, hiding it while a sheet is open or the control is scrolled out of sight.
- `Views/RandomTips/`: the random tip's card at the bottom of the sidebar, with Next tip, close, and a right-click menu that also turns tips off, and each tip's copy. It steps aside while the onboarding tour or a first-use tip shows.
- `Views/Indicators/`: a session's status glyphs, the working spinner among them, the pin, the archive box, and the hourglass of a session deleted when its tab closes.
- `Views/SessionActions/`: copying ids and paths, and showing files in Finder, for the menus.
- `Views/Settings/`: the Settings sheet, with General, Appearance, Permissions, and Help pages, each in a `SettingsTabPage` of the one size they share. General holds language, startup, tabs (what closing one and ⌘-clicking a file in one do, in `General/Tabs/`), whether the sidebar shows subagents, and notifications, then About: the app and macOS versions, software updates, and links to send feedback, report an issue, or open the website and GitHub. `Models/App/Feedback/` opens the Guide's feedback form or a GitHub issue with those versions, from About or the Help menu; `CrashReports/` there reads what a macOS crash report says about a crash of the app and fills a bug report or email with it.
- `Views/Settings/Appearance/`: the app's appearance and theme in `App/`, with the editor for the theme's colors in `App/Customization/`, then its terminals' colors, font, and preview in `Terminal/`, with the color scheme and iTerm2 import menus in `Terminal/Colors/`.
- `Views/Settings/Permissions/`: each permission's status and a link to its page in System Settings.
- `Models/Localization/`, `Services/Localization/`, `Views/Localization/`: resource-driven language selection, native string resolution, and the shared SwiftUI locale. See [localization](localization.md).
- `Localization/Localizable.xcstrings`: the authoritative String Catalog. `Sources/JustSessions/Resources/Localization/` contains generated SwiftPM resources.
- `Views/Settings/Help/`: the Help page, one `HelpSection` each: the tour and user guide with the switch for the sidebar's random tip, how each part of the window works, SSH setup, what to check when a session is missing, and the keyboard shortcut list (`Shortcuts/`). `Models/App/AppLinks.swift` holds the destinations of Help and About.
- `Views/HelpPopover/`: the ? beside a heading or control that opens its explanation in a popover, in Settings, the New session sheet, and the archived projects sheet.
- `Views/Theme/`: the values every view draws with, grouped by kind: `Colors/` (the chosen theme's `ThemePalette`), `Typography/` (`ThemeFont` and `ThemeGlyphSize`), `Shapes/` (`ThemeCornerRadius`), `Buttons/` and `Controls/` (button, checkbox, field, and divider styles), `SegmentedPicker/` (`ThemeSegmentedPicker`, used in place of the system segmented picker, whose selected segment barely shows on dark theme surfaces), `Sheets/` (`ThemeSheetLayout`), `Elevation/` (`ThemeShadow`), and `Motion/` (`ThemeMotion`). The contract tests in `Tests/JustSessionsTests/Contracts/UIStyle/` hold views to them; see [UI style](ui-style.md).
- `Views/Branding/`: the app mark drawn in the sidebar header.
- `Views/Branding/Providers/`: each CLI's icon. Codex, Kiro, and OpenCode draw their own template images on a 16-point grid; the rest are SF Symbols.
