# Split view

[Back to requirements](../README.md)

Split view follows Chrome's `TabStripModel`. Rules are pure functions in `TerminalTabStrip`, `TerminalSplitLayout`, and `TerminalSplitPair`.

| ID | Requirement | Origin | Related tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

The selected tab in no split offers Split right and Split down in place of Add tab to new split view (SPL-20). The Tabs menu offers Add Tab to New Split View (⌥⌘N) while the selected tab is in no split, and Separate Views, Close Left View and Close Right View (Close Top View and Close Bottom View when stacked), Reverse Views, and Stack Views or Place Views Side by Side for the shown split or chooser. *Changed 31 October 2026: stacked splits (SPL-18) and Split right and Split down (SPL-20).* | `9cd4b9e`, `457652d`, `08a45bc` | `TerminalSplitStoreTests` |
