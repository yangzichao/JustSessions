Good to know.

Which features work with my CLI?

All six support local browsing and resume. Preview, branching, deletion, and SSH support vary. Full compatibility.

What do I need?

macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon, with your CLI installed and signed in. SSH needs passwordless access, rsync, and the CLI on the host. Persistence needs tmux 3.3+ locally, or tmux on the remote host. Keep the machine awake.

Is it free?

Yes. No JustSessions account or subscription. Your CLI provider’s charges still apply.

Where is my history stored?

JustSessions reads your CLI files and caches SSH history locally. It does not upload conversations to a JustSessions service. Storage & privacy.
