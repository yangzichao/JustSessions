Good to know.

Which features work with my CLI?

Every CLI supports browsing, reading, resume, deletion, and SSH. In-app branching varies. Full compatibility.

What do I need?

macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon, with your CLI installed and signed in. Keep your existing configuration and accounts. Remote setup.

Can I change the language and appearance?

Follow System, English, or 简体中文. Six themes, light or dark, and terminal fonts. Changes apply in place. Settings.

Is it free?

Yes. No JustSessions account or subscription. Your CLI provider’s charges still apply.

Where is my history stored?

JustSessions reads your CLI files and caches SSH history locally. It does not upload conversations to a JustSessions service. Storage & privacy.
